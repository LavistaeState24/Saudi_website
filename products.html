Not sure which structure fits your project?

As a general guide: small and medium farms typically start with plastic or shade-net structures for cost-effectiveness. Commercial vegetable operations targeting export or retail chains usually move to polycarbonate or glass hydroponic systems for consistent year-round yield.

Quick guide

  • •Small/medium plastic greenhouses → cost-effective, fast to install
  • •High-tech glass/hydroponic → commercial-scale, export-ready
  • •Large-scale production → turnkey glass hydroponic farms
  • •Budget-friendly polyhouses → shade-net & fiberglass options
