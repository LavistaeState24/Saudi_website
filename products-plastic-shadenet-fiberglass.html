Structure types

Arched Plastic Greenhouses

Curved-frame single units with UV-treated plastic sheeting, suited to most vegetable and herb crops.

Upright Plastic Greenhouses

Straight-wall structures offering more usable vertical space for taller crops or equipment.

Shade-Net Structures

Nets in green, beige or black densities to manage sun exposure for shade-tolerant crops and nurseries.

Fiberglass Greenhouses

Corrugated fiberglass sheeting for improved durability and light diffusion versus plastic film.

Single Air-Conditioned Units

Individually cooled single structures for higher-value crops needing tighter temperature control.

Built with hydraulic press-formed steel

Our frames are formed using hydraulic presses rather than welded joints, improving consistency and reducing long-term corrosion points — an important factor for structures operating in high-heat, high-UV conditions.
