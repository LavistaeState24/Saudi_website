Optional Automation

A step up in durability and control

Polycarbonate structures hold up better to UV exposure and impact than plastic sheeting, while air-conditioning extends the range of crops that can be grown reliably through peak summer heat.

Best suited for

Growers producing higher-value crops, or those in regions with more extreme summer temperatures.

Lifespan

Polycarbonate panels typically outlast plastic film, reducing recovering/re-sheeting frequency.

Upgrade path

Structures can be specified with control panels ready for later automation upgrades.
