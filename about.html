About Basateen

Three decades of Saudi greenhouse craftsmanship, one growing group.

Basateen brings together local manufacturing heritage, turnkey project expertise and large-scale hydroponic operations to serve growers of every size across the Kingdom.

Our story

What began as a specialised greenhouse factory in the Qassim region has grown into a full-service group covering manufacturing, turnkey construction and hydroponic farm operation. Over 35 years, our teams have built structures ranging from small shade-net tunnels for individual farmers to multi-hectare glass facilities producing for export.

Today, Basateen combines hydraulic press-formed steel manufacturing with partnerships from leading Dutch and European greenhouse technology firms, giving clients a single point of contact regardless of project scale.

Mission, vision & values

Mission

To make reliable, climate-appropriate greenhouse technology accessible to growers across Saudi Arabia, at every scale.

Vision

A Kingdom where controlled-environment agriculture is a mainstay of food security and export capability.

Values

Engineering quality, water responsibility, and long-term partnership with the farms and communities we serve.
