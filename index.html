Built for desert conditions, not adapted to them.

Saudi Arabia's climate calls for specialised cooling, water recycling and energy management — not greenhouse designs borrowed from temperate regions. Our structures and systems are selected and engineered specifically for high heat, low humidity and water-scarce conditions.

Climate control

Evaporative cooling pads, circulation fans and electronic shade curtains regulate internal temperature through peak summer months.

Water efficiency

Closed-loop hydroponic systems recycle irrigation water, cutting consumption dramatically versus open-field methods.

Local + international expertise

Saudi manufacturing capability paired with Dutch and European greenhouse technology partners for large-scale projects.
