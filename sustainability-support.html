Sustainability & Support

Growing responsibly, with the Kingdom's support.

Controlled-environment agriculture plays a direct role in Vision 2030's food security and water-conservation goals — and government financing can help make projects viable.

Sustainability & food security

Water Conservation

Hydroponic systems use an estimated 70–90% less water than open-field farming methods.

Year-Round Production

Controlled environments remove seasonal limitations, supporting consistent domestic supply.

Vision 2030 Alignment

Greenhouse agriculture supports national goals around food security and reduced import reliance.

Financing & government support

Agricultural Development Fund

Financing programs supporting the establishment and expansion of agricultural facilities, including greenhouses.

Ministry of Environment, Water & Agriculture

Incentive programs supporting sustainable and water-efficient farming practices.

Market growth

The Controlled Environment Agriculture market in Saudi Arabia continues to grow at a high compound annual rate, supporting long-term project viability.
