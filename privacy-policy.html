02 Scope & applicability

This Privacy Policy governs the processing of personal data collected through our web portal, digital forms, custom quotation submissions, and technical support inquiries within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia and internationally.

03 Legal basis for processing

We process personal data in accordance with statutory grounds specified under the Saudi PDPL:

  • Explicit consent: Granted when submitting quote requests, contacting technical teams, or opting into newsletters.
  • Contractual performance: Processing necessary to prepare engineering proposals, fulfill commercial agreements, or execute supply contracts.
  • Legal mandates: Compliance with Saudi tax rules, commercial regulations, and SDAIA directives.
  • Legitimate interest: Ensuring system security, preventing fraudulent activity, and optimizing website architecture.

04 Types of personal data collected

We strictly gather data essential to fulfilling commercial interactions and engineering services:

  • Contact identifiers: Full name, business email, phone number, and physical office or farm site address.
  • Commercial information: Company registration details, project size, greenhouse system parameters, and geographic location.
  • Technical device data: IP address, browser specification, device operating system, and system usage logs.
  • Inquiry records: Inbound message logs, quote attachments, email exchanges, and consultation notes.

05 Methods of data collection

Data is gathered directly when users complete digital inquiry forms, email our technical team, or request custom engineering quotes. Automated collection occurs through cookie protocols when navigating our website pages.

06 Purposes of data processing

Collected personal data is utilized exclusively for defined operational goals:

  • Designing, costing, and issuing customized commercial greenhouse quotations.
  • Fulfilling client support requests and technical consultation scheduling.
  • Meeting legal, regulatory, tax, and auditing obligations under Saudi jurisdiction.
  • Safeguarding our web infrastructure and monitoring network usability.

07 Data retention & storage

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to achieve the operational or legal purposes outlined, or as required by Saudi commercial retention laws. Upon expiration of the retention timeframe, data is permanently erased or anonymized.

08 Security & encryption standards

We implement technical safeguards—including TLS 1.3 protocol encryption, role-based access restrictions, active firewalls, and regular vulnerability audits—aligned with SDAIA and National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA) directives.

09 Data subject rights (PDPL)

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, individuals are entitled to the following rights:

  • Right to access & information: Receive confirmation of processing and obtain a readable copy of your personal data records.
  • Right to correction: Request updates or corrections to incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to erasure: Request destruction of personal records when operational necessity expires or consent is withdrawn.
  • Right to revoke consent: Withdraw processing consent at any time without affecting past legal processing.

10 Cookies & tracking technologies

Cookies are utilized to save website preferences and capture non-identifiable browser analytics. You can adjust your browser settings to decline non-essential cookies, though doing so may affect interactive features.

11 Third-party data sharing

We do not sell or monetize personal information. Data is shared exclusively with vetted data processors (e.g., cloud hosts, logistics contractors) under strict non-disclosure terms, or when legally compelled by Saudi judicial bodies.

12 Cross-border data transfers

Personal data primary storage resides on secure infrastructure within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Any external transfer adheres strictly to SDAIA regulations governing international data handling.

13 Direct marketing & communications

Marketing communications are sent exclusively to users who express opt-in consent. Every email contains a clear link allowing immediate opt-out from future mailings.

14 Protection of minors

Our website services target commercial and business entities. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from persons under 18 years of age without explicit guardian authorization.
